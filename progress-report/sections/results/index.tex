\section{RESULTS AND ANALYSIS}

This section reports the results of the baseline implementation described in the Implementation chapter, obtained under the clean-audio evaluation protocols defined for the classical and deep learning baselines: all ablation results are computed on the ASVspoof5 development partition, and the evaluation partition is reported exactly once, in the final baseline comparison (Section~6.4), consistent with the train/development/evaluation discipline followed throughout. Results involving RTC-induced distortion (codec compression, packet loss, receiver-side enhancement) are outside the scope of this progress report and are deferred to the final report, once the RTC-aware augmentation pipeline is integrated.

The subsections below mirror the ablation structure introduced for the classical baselines: feature representation, duration matching, and the XGBoost regularization sweep, followed by the overall comparison across all eight baseline models. Numeric entries marked \textit{TBD} correspond to evaluation runs that are complete in pipeline but not yet finalized at the time of writing; they will be populated with measured values before submission of the final report.
