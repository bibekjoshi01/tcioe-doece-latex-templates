\section{RESULTS AND ANALYSIS}

This section reports the results of the baseline implementation described in the Implementation chapter, obtained under the clean-audio evaluation protocols defined for the classical and deep learning baselines. All ablation results are computed on the ASVspoof5 development partition; the evaluation partition is touched exactly once, in the final baseline comparison (Section~6.4), consistent with the train/development/evaluation discipline followed throughout. Results involving RTC-induced distortion (codec compression, packet loss, receiver-side enhancement) are outside the scope of this progress report and are deferred to the final report, once the RTC-aware augmentation pipeline is integrated.
