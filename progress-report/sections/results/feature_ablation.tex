\subsection{Feature Representation Comparison}

A systematic comparison was conducted on the development set (20,000-utterance stratified training subset) to select the feature representation used by the four classical classifiers, across two axes: feature source and WavLM configuration.

Table~\ref{tab:feature_source_ablation} compares handcrafted acoustic features, WavLM embeddings, and their concatenation, as inputs to each classical model (WavLM base checkpoint, last-layer pooling).

\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.4}
\setlength{\tabcolsep}{8pt}
\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\caption{Feature-Source Comparison (Development Set EER, \%)}
\label{tab:feature_source_ablation}
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{4.3cm} *{4}{>{\centering\arraybackslash}X}}
\hline
\textbf{Feature Source} & \textbf{XGBoost} & \textbf{Random Forest} & \textbf{SVM} & \textbf{GBM} \\
\hline
Handcrafted-only & 44.80 & 32.25 & 33.96 & 30.82 \\
WavLM-only & 20.88 & 26.41 & 19.08 & 21.12 \\
Concatenated (handcrafted + WavLM) & 26.54 & 23.28 & 22.58 & 23.56 \\
\hline
\end{tabularx}
\end{table}

Handcrafted acoustic features were the weakest representation across every model, by a wide margin. Concatenating them with WavLM embeddings only helped Random Forest; for XGBoost, GBM, and SVM, concatenation performed worse than WavLM alone, suggesting the lower-dimensional handcrafted block added noise that these models did not discount as well as Random Forest's per-tree feature subsampling does.

Table~\ref{tab:wavlm_config_ablation} compares three of the four candidate WavLM configurations (base vs.\ large checkpoint; last-layer vs.\ all-layer-averaged pooling), using the WavLM-only feature source established above. The base-checkpoint, all-layer-averaged configuration was not evaluated, as the large-checkpoint results below were already sufficient to choose the final selection.

The improvement from the base to the large WavLM checkpoint (last-layer pooling) accounts for most of the overall gain, reducing EER by 5--10 points across all four models. All-layer-averaged pooling on top of the large checkpoint adds a further, smaller improvement of 3--5 points across all models. Both changes were kept, and WavLM-large with all-layer-averaged pooling was adopted as the final representation.
