\subsection{Motivation}

The increasing accessibility of AI-based voice synthesis tools has significantly lowered the barrier for generating convincing synthetic speech, making voice-based attacks more practical and scalable than ever before. The miuse for malicious purposes poses emerging threats and challenges to security. Current detection systems are primarily designed for offline analysis using clean audio datasets and often fail to maintain reliability under realistic communication environments.

This project is motivated by the need to bridge the gap between laboratory-level deepfake detection research and practical real-world deployment in communication systems. By focusing on real-time detection under degraded VoIP conditions, the project aims to investigate the robustness, feasibility, and performance trade-offs of synthetic speech detection systems in practical communication scenarios.
