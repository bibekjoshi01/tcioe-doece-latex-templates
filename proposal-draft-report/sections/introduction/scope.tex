\subsection{Scope and Application}

The project focuses on audio-based synthetic speech detection in VoIP communication systems under real-world degraded communication conditions. The system will analyze streaming audio segments and perform near real-time inference using machine learning and signal processing techniques.

\subsubsection{Project Capabilities}

The project is capable of the following:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Detect whether incoming speech audio is real or AI-generated (synthetic speech) in near real-time
    \item Processes streaming audio chunks instead of requiring full audio files
    \item Includes a simulation environment for testing VoIP-like audio transmission
    \item Supports low-latency inference pipeline suitable for real-time communication scenarios
    \item Evaluates and compares multiple models/approaches for robustness (e.g., ML vs DL models)
    \item Provide measurable detection confidence and performance metrics under different scenarios
\end{itemize}


\subsubsection{Project Limitations}

The scope of this project is subject to the following limitations:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Performance may degrade when exposed to unseen or highly advanced neural codec-based synthetic speech models
    \item Limited generalization to languages or accents not well represented in training data (e.g., Nepali-specific datasets may be limited)
    \item Accuracy may reduce under extreme noise conditions or highly compressed audio streams
    \item System is not designed for large-scale production deployment
    \item Real-time performance depends on hardware capability and model complexity trade-offs
    \item Dataset availability constraints may limit coverage of diverse real-world VoIP scenarios and attack types
\end{itemize}


\subsubsection{Project Applications}

The potential applications of the proposed system includes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Fraud and Social Engineering Prevention:} Detects voice spoofing in phishing calls, impersonation scams, and financial fraud attempts
    \item \textbf{Secure VoIP Communication:} Enhances security in internet-based calling systems (e.g., Zoom-like platforms, SIP-based VoIP systems) by flagging synthetic speech in real-time
    \item \textbf{Voice Authentication Systems:} Acts as a countermeasure layer for speaker verification systems used in banking, telecom, and identity verification services
    \item \textbf{Call Center Security:} Helps verify authenticity of customer or agent voices in customer support and outsourcing environments
    \item \textbf{Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics:} Assists in analyzing suspicious audio recordings for potential AI-generated manipulation
    \item \textbf{Online Meeting Integrity:} Supports detection of synthetic or manipulated voices during virtual meetings and remote collaboration tools
    \item \textbf{Media and Content Verification:} Helps verify authenticity of audio content used in news, interviews, and public broadcasts
\end{itemize}
